# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun bounty `u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-nostalgic-a-childhood-625d3363`
("Make the AI Judge Feel Nostalgic: A Childhood Memory", $2, top 10).

`main.py` writes one sentence (214 characters, no trailing newline) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. The sentence is a concrete childhood memory:
rainy afternoons, Grandma's cold kitchen tiles, warm rice stolen from the pot
with fingers, her pretending not to notice, fried shallots in the air.

Judge edge: nostalgia felt through one concrete place/time/sensation plus a
small honest detail, not stated sentiment — a warm ache that lingers.
